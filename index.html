How I Think

The Last Message

Screenplay · In Development

THE QUESTION UNDERNEATH

“What happens to the words people were saving for later, and never sent?”

the seed
A conversation about grief. Someone mentioned they still texted their dead father's number sometimes. Not expecting a reply. Just because the habit had not stopped.
the question
What if the reply came?
the material
News stories about grief tech startups. The quiet industry forming around loss and digital presence. None of it felt like the real thing yet.
the pivot
The first draft made the service the villain. That was too easy. The real question was not whether it was ethical. It was whether the son deserved to know which messages were real. The script got harder once the villain disappeared.
the thing
Registered screenplay. 18 pages. SWA India, Feb 2026.

WHAT I DO

available, selectively

01. Creative Concepts — IDEATION

“A great execution can’t save a small idea.”

So I start where the work should start... with the idea! Campaigns, launches, narratives, formats — finding the thought that makes everything that follows more interesting.

02. Writing & Development — NARRATIVE DESIGN & STRUCTURAL BLUEPRINTING

“An idea can only travel as far as its words.”

I find the words, structure and voice that let an idea become what it wants to be; whether it’s a line, a script, a film, a series or something that doesn’t have a name yet.

03. Brand Building — STRATEGY & POSITIONING

“A brand becomes what it keeps doing.”

So I help make those choices count: from positioning and voice to campaigns, launches and the creative decisions that, over time, become the brand.

04. Film Direction — CREATIVE LEADERSHIP & EXECUTION

“We believe stories when we believe the people in them.”

Long-form nonfiction taught me to sense the story that’s already there - in people, in behavior, and in the moments you could never have written.

05. Creative Advisory — CONSULTING & GUIDANCE

“The closer you are to the work, the less you see.”

Sometimes what a project needs isn’t more work. It’s another way of seeing it. I bring an experienced outside eye to ideas, writing, brands, campaigns and production, especially when something isn’t quite working and nobody can say why.
